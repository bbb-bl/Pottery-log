Pottery Log – Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Pottery Log is a free, non-commercial app for logging ceramic pieces. It is made by Botond for himself and his classmates. This page explains what data the app keeps and why.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address and password. Login is handled by Supabase, which stores passwords only in hashed form. Nobody can read your password, including the app's owner.
  • Your log: the pieces, photos, glazes, notes, firings and settings you enter. They are stored with Supabase and protected so that only your account can read or change them.

Google Drive (optional)

If you tap "Connect Google Drive", the app asks Google for the drive.file permission. With it the app can create a "Pottery Log" folder in your Drive and save a copy of your pieces and photos there. It can only see and change files it created itself, never any of your other Drive files.

The Google connection stays only in your browser and lasts about an hour. It is not stored on any server. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already copied to your Drive stay there and belong to you.

"Name it for me" (optional)

When you tap "Name it for me", up to two small copies of the piece's photos and its details are sent to Google's Gemini service to suggest names. The app uses Gemini's free tier, so Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Don't use this button if you don't want that.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone, except the services named above that the app needs to work.

Deleting your data

You can delete pieces in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and everything stored with it, open an issue at github.com/bbb-bl/Pottery-log/issues or contact Botond directly. It will be removed.

Questions

Contact Botond through GitHub, or ask him in class.
